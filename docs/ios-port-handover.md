# iOS: port the November 2026 rebuild from Android

Written on a Windows machine that cannot compile Swift. The Android app in `android/` was rebuilt, compiled and checked first; it is the reference. Port each piece below to `ios/Slate`, build in Xcode, check it in the Simulator at iPhone SE, iPhone 16 and iPad widths, then archive for TestFlight.

Read `AGENTS.md` and `DESIGN.md` first. Mint (`SlatePalette.accent`) is the only accent. No label sits above a heading anywhere.

## Server (already in this branch, needs deploying before the apps can use it)

- `GET /api/v1/library?nudges=1` adds `newEpisodes: [{ titleId, title, posterPath, season, episode, episodeName, airDate }]`.
- `GET /api/v1/discover/home` returns `rails: [{ id, title, items: [{ tmdbId, mediaType, title, posterPath, releaseDate, saved }] }]`. It existed already (built for the TV app).
- `GET /api/v1/slate-lists/{slug}` returns `{ slug, name, description, curator, titles: [rail item] }`; `POST` copies the list into the library and returns `{ added, listId }`.
- Shapes are in `contracts/openapi.yaml`. Kotlin models: `android/app/src/main/java/space/s1ate/app/model/Models.kt` (`NewEpisode`, `DiscoverRail`, `DiscoverRailItem`, `CuratedList`).

## What to port, with the Android file to copy behaviour from

All paths are under `android/app/src/main/java/space/s1ate/app/ui/`. Rendered references: run `./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest` in `android/`, then open `android/app/build/shots/*.png`.

1. **Liquid selector** (`LiquidSelector.kt`). One body in the accent colour that stretches as it travels: its leading edge follows a fast spring, its trailing edge a slower one. Springs, as stiffness and damping at mass 1: controls 380/29 (lead) and 235/26 (tail); dock 250/23 and 150/21. In SwiftUI these map directly to `.interpolatingSpring(stiffness:damping:)`. Each option is as wide as its label plus an equal share of the spare room. The chosen label's ink colour is drawn by the selector: render the labels twice and mask the ink copy with the body's shape. Draggable as well as tappable; past either end it gives 30% then resists. The body thins by up to 16% while stretched. With Reduce Motion on, it jumps. Replace the `matchedGeometryEffect` pill in `MainTabView.swift` and the 2 px indicator in `SlateChrome.swift` with it.
2. **Dock and top bar** (`SlateChrome.kt`). Tabs become Library, Discover, Lists inside one liquid selector (60 pt tall, icon over an 11 pt label), with a 60 pt round add button beside it (plus icon in the accent). It floats over the content, 76 pt tall, max 430 pt wide. The top bar keeps the wordmark and the avatar; the dead moon icon and the search icon go (the add button opens search). Delete `SlateSectionHeader`'s eyebrow: screens use a plain 30 pt bold title, and the Library has no title at all.
3. **Library** (`LibraryScreen.kt`). One screen replaces the three shelf tabs. Row: shelf selector (All, Up Next, Watching, Watched; fills the row, max 420 pt) and a 40 pt Filters button that turns accent-tinted and shows a count once any filter is set. Default shelf is Up Next. Grid: posters only, three per row on a phone (more when wide, about 132 pt each), 10 pt gaps, 16 pt gutters, 12 pt corners; a reaction shows as a small badge. No quick-action buttons on posters. Long-press reordering only on a single shelf with "Your order" and no filters. "All" interleaves the shelves one from each in turn. A change of shelf or filter fades the grid up from 45% over 220 ms.
4. **Filters sheet** (`FilterPanel` in `LibraryScreen.kt`). A bottom sheet (`.sheet` with `.presentationDetents`), in this order: Format (All, Films, Series: liquid selector with icons), Genre (wrapping chips, by genre id), Year (Any, 2020s, 2010s, 2000s, Older: liquid selector), Reaction (All, Loved, Liked, Disliked: only on Watched), Sort by (Your order, Recently added or Recently watched, Highest rated, Newest release: rows with a tick). Pinned at the bottom: a full-width accent button reading "Show N titles", "Show 1 title" or "No titles match". "Clear all" appears in the header once a filter is set and resets Format, Genre, Year and Reaction only, never the shelf or the order. Filtering rules: `filterAndSort` in the same file (a port of `lib/filter-utils.ts`).
5. **Library prompts** (`LibraryNudges.kt`). Above the grid, scrolling with it: a "New episodes" poster row (from `newEpisodes`; label "S5 E3 out Tue" with the season and episode in the accent); under five titles, a card with five slots and two equal buttons (Find titles, Add from a link); ten titles and no lists, a card offering "Make <Genre> night your first list" (`suggestList`). Cards follow `DESIGN.md`: close button pinned top-right, actions in one row of equal widths, a single action spanning the row, 44 pt buttons. Dismissals persist (use `UserDefaults`).
6. **Discover tab** (`DiscoverHome.kt`). Rails from `discover/home`: a 17 pt semibold rail title, then a horizontal row of 116 pt posters; a tick badge marks saved titles. Tapping opens the existing catalogue detail. Cache the rails for the session. While loading, show the rails' own shape as placeholders, not a spinner.
7. **Lists made by Slate** (`CuratedListScreen` in `DiscoverHome.kt`). Opened from `slate://l/<slug>` (add to `handleOpenURL`) and, once the Associated Domains entitlement and the site's `apple-app-site-association` file exist, from `https://s1ate.space/l/<slug>`. Name as the heading, "A list by Slate" under it in the accent, the description, one full-width "Add to your slate" button, then the poster grid.
8. **Add from a link** (`ImportScreen` in `CollectionScreens.kt`). No longer a tab: opened from the search screen and from the share extension. One pill field with a Find button inside it, a line naming the source ("7 found in <source>"), then the found titles as a poster grid where each poster carries its own state (plus badge, accent tick once saved). Bottom button: "Add all N titles".
9. **Profile** (`ProfileScreenAdvanced` in `CollectionScreens.kt`). Avatar and editable name as the headline, a row of three shelf counts, then one grouped card: share toggle, the public link, sign out. No "PUBLIC" label.
10. **Remove frameXTV** from `TitleDetailView.swift` (the "Watch on frameXTV" link near line 236 and `frameXTVURL` near line 534). The web removed it in commit `4d0f130`; it must not ship to testers.
11. **Wording**: "Watchlist" and "Want" become "Up Next"; "Sentiment" becomes "Reaction"; "Love/Like/Dislike" filter labels become "Loved/Liked/Disliked". Uppercase monospaced labels ("PEOPLE", "LIST", "FILM") become normal sentence-case text; a title's type, year and runtime sit under its name, not above it.
12. **Arrival**: a screen that replaces another fades up from 45% over 220 ms with `cubic-bezier(0.33, 1, 0.68, 1)`; images fade in when loaded (iOS already does this through `CachedAsyncPhaseImage`). Pressable things scale to 0.97 (`SlatePressStyle` already does).

## Release (TestFlight)

1. In `ios/project.yml`: `MARKETING_VERSION: "0.2.0"`, bump `CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION` for every upload, then `xcodegen generate`.
2. Needs an active Apple Developer Program membership. In Xcode choose the team for both targets (`Slate` and `SlateShare`), let it manage signing, and register `space.s1ate.app` and `space.s1ate.app.share`.
3. Create the app record in App Store Connect (bundle id `space.s1ate.app`), then Product > Archive > Distribute App > TestFlight. Internal testers can install as soon as processing ends; external testers need Beta App Review.
4. The app signs in with Google only. App Review requires Sign in with Apple beside any third-party sign-in before a public release; the server side is ready (`docs/native-apps.md`, "Apple setup"). It is not needed for internal TestFlight testing.
